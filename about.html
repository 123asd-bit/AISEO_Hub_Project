ABOUT THE PROJECT

SEO learning, made practical

AISEO Hub is an educational web project created by Team Codecrafters to demonstrate clean site architecture, on-page SEO best practices, beginner-friendly tutorials, and interactive browser tools.

Our purpose

Many students, new bloggers, and small business owners struggle to move from abstract SEO advice to practical execution. AISEO Hub brings lightweight planning tools and clear step-by-step guides together in one place so beginners can draft headlines, write concise meta descriptions, brainstorm long-tail keyword ideas, and audit page essentials without complex paid suites.

How to use the tools

Every generator on AISEO Hub runs locally in your browser to produce structured starting drafts. Outputs are not a substitute for real search-intent research, fact-checking, or live keyword metrics—use them alongside Google Search Console, Google Analytics, and manual SERP inspection.

Explore our guides

Start with SEO Basics for Beginners to learn how AI SEO and search engines work, follow our Keyword Research Workflow, or review the On-Page SEO Checklist before publishing your next page.
